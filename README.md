# 🏋️‍♂️ FitForge

is a fully **offline fitness application** designed to help users achieve their fitness goals, whether it's **bulking (weight gain)** or **cutting (weight loss)**.  
The app provides a complete body assessment, calculates daily nutritional needs, and tracks physical activity.  

---

## 🎯 Key Features  
- **Body Assessment**: Input (weight, height, age, gender) to calculate BMI, BMR, and daily caloric needs.  
- **Clear Goals**: Choose between bulking or cutting and get a tailored plan.  
- **Nutrition**:  
  - Calculate daily requirements of protein, carbs, healthy fats, fiber, and water.  
  - A large food library with nutritional values.  
- **Workouts**:  
  - Exercise library categorized by target muscle group (Chest – Back – Shoulders – Arms – Legs – Abs).  
  - Ready-made training programs for beginners, intermediates, and advanced athletes.  
  - Recovery tips and injury prevention guidelines.  
- **Activity Tracking**:  
  - Record daily steps.  
  - Track calories burned.  
  - Progress reports with charts and tables.  
- **Knowledge Base**: Articles about sleep, supplements, benefits of natural foods, and general fitness & nutrition tips.  

---

## 🛠️ App Features  
- Works **completely offline** with no account or internet required.  
- Modern and clean design with a user-friendly interface.  
- Data stored locally on the device (no server needed).  

---

## 📂 App Sections  
1. **Home** – Quick overview of goals and progress.  
2. **Personal Assessment** – Input data & calculate requirements.  
3. **Nutrition** – Daily needs + food library + diet plans.  
4. **Workouts** – Exercise library + training programs.  
5. **Activity** – Steps & calories burned tracking.  
6. **Knowledge** – Articles & general tips.  

---

✨ **FitForge** is designed to be a complete reference for athletes and fitness enthusiasts, with a modern and calculated approach, a smooth user experience, and no internet required.
